About CI Lab

A place to learn, build and explore intelligent computing.

The Computational Intelligence Lab at South Asian University provides a platform for students, faculty and researchers interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science and related areas.

The website brings important lab information, projects, resources and announcements together in one easy-to-use platform.


Learn More About CI Lab

What We Do

We support learning and research in computational intelligence by providing opportunities to work on projects, explore resources and collaborate with other students and researchers.


Our goal is to create a simple digital platform where the CI Lab community can easily find the information they need.
